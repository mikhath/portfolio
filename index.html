MI KHA / SELECTED WORK

Campaign · Gamification · Team project / 2023

CampaignGraphic DesignGamificationTeam Project

Back-2-School

A five-day interactive campaign for the goSupermodel community.

Inspired by visual novels and life simulation games, players collectively controlled one student character. Daily choices shaped the story and earned reputation points towards a shared reward. Across the Visual, Story and Coding teams, my role included coordinating the project, developing the concept and supporting events, designing digital assets, and helping shape the creative direction.

Community feedback highlighted excitement and a sense of connection. The format later became a template for platform-run events.
